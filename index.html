How to Play

Daily Challenge

Every day at midnight, a new challenge begins with 10 sentences that get progressively harder.

How It Works

Read a sentence with one word hidden. Choose the definition that best fits the missing word based on context clues. You never see the word until after you answer!

The Challenge

  • Level 1-3: Easy everyday words
  • Level 4-7: Medium college-level words
  • Level 8-10: Hard advanced vocabulary
  • ❌ One wrong answer ends the game!

Example

Sentence: "The detective's _____ nature helped solve the case."

Choose the definition that fits:

  • A) Showing great attention to detail
  • B) Feeling extreme sadness

Answer: A - The word was "meticulous"

Tips

  • Look for context clues in the sentence
  • Consider the tone and meaning
  • Eliminate definitions that don't fit
  • One mistake ends the challenge!
